Nimbus Service Level Agreement

Nimbus guarantees 99.9% uptime for Pro and Team plans, measured monthly.
If uptime falls below this guarantee, affected customers receive service credits
equal to 10% of their monthly bill for each additional 0.1% of downtime.
Free plan users are not covered by the uptime guarantee.
Scheduled maintenance windows are announced at least 48 hours in advance
and do not count against the uptime guarantee.
