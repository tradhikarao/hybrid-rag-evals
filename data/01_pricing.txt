Nimbus Cloud Storage Pricing

Nimbus offers three plans: Free, Pro, and Team.
The Free plan includes 5GB of storage and is limited to 2 devices.
The Pro plan costs $9.99 per month and includes 2TB of storage, unlimited devices,
and priority email support.
The Team plan costs $24.99 per month per user and includes 5TB of pooled storage,
shared folders, and admin controls.
Annual billing for Pro or Team gives a 15% discount compared to monthly billing.
